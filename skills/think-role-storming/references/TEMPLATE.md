# Persona-Tagged Idea List - Template

Fill this in. The deliverable is the persona-tagged idea list - the generative question, the chosen personas with their distancing rationale, the candidate ideas each attributed to a persona, and a hand-off note - not a prose essay and not a recommendation.

> This is a DIVERGENT pool. Do NOT rank, score, or down-select inside this artifact; that is a separate convergence step. Every persona must be uninhibited or deliberately foreign - an inhibited persona (a cautious auditor, a risk-averse executive, a rigid bureaucrat) suppresses ideas instead of freeing them.
