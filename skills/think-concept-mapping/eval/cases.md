# Eval cases: think-concept-mapping

> Trigger and output evaluation, derived from `SKILL.md` and `evidence/dossier.md`. No runner yet (deferred to the Silver climb); check by hand or wire in later.

## Should trigger

- "Map out how all these pieces of our pricing and growth model actually connect to each other."
- "I have a tangle of concepts about our onboarding - lay out how they relate so I can see the gaps."
- "Build a concept map of the free-tier decision space showing how each idea links to the others."
- "We keep saying these things are 'related' but never say how. Make the relationships explicit."
- "Where are the missing links and false assumptions in how we think churn connects to support and pricing?"
- "Externalize how these domain concepts interrelate, with every connection named, not just lines between boxes."

## Should NOT trigger (wrong tool / near-miss)

- "Is the argument for launching the free tier sound? Find the weak inference." (near-miss: argument-mapping - judging soundness of one claim, which a concept map cannot do)
- "Break 'why is churn rising?' into a MECE set of sub-questions." (issue-tree - top-down decomposition)
- "Cluster these 200 user-research notes into themes." (affinity-mapping - bottom-up clustering, no named relationships)
- "Why does this problem keep recurring - take it down to root structures and mental models." (iceberg-model - fixed causal levels)
- "Make me a quick mind map to brainstorm free-tier ideas." (excluded Buzan mind-mapping - unlabeled branches; not in this library)
- "Summarize how our product works for the board." (summarization)

## Output checks (a good output must)

- [ ] Connect concepts with named, directed linking phrases; no unlabeled associations.
- [ ] Make each node-link-node triple read as a complete, defensible proposition (a sentence).
- [ ] Include at least one cross-link connecting concepts across different clusters.
- [ ] List gaps (under-connected concepts), missing links, and questionable propositions explicitly.
- [ ] Be a non-hierarchical network, not an argument tree (no claim/co-premise/objection) or a MECE decomposition.
- [ ] Be the concept-map artifact, not prose; not claim to improve learning, retention, or decisions.

## Value vs unaided baseline

Asked to relate a set of domain concepts, a strong model unaided tends to emit a fluent paragraph or a list of "X is related to Y" adjacencies in which the *kind* of relationship is never specified - exactly the vagueness the labeled-link constraint exists to break. This skill forces every connection to be a named proposition and demands cross-links, which is what surfaces the contested junction and the unclosed loop in the Northwind run. Note the honest scope in `evidence/dossier.md`: the large human meta-analyses (Nesbit & Adesope 2006; Schroeder et al. 2018, g=0.58) measure knowledge *retention*, an outcome that does not transfer to an agent, so the claimed value is the construction-time gap-surfacing only, not better learning or decisions - which is why this skill is graded M/P, not S, despite a meta-analytic base larger than the S-graded argument-mapping.
