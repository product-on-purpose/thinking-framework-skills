# Eval cases: think-causal-layered-analysis

> Trigger and output evaluation, derived from `SKILL.md` and `evidence/dossier.md`. No runner yet (a harness is deferred); these are the cases to check by hand, or to wire into evals later.

## Should trigger

- "We've fought about 'free vs paid' for two years and every planning cycle re-runs the exact same arguments. The numbers aren't the real problem - we disagree about what the company is *for*. Read the issue at its layers and rebuild it so we can see it differently."
- "This policy debate is completely stuck. Both sides have argued the statistics and the mechanics to death; the real clash is in unstated worldviews and the story underneath. Take it down to its deepest metaphor and reconstruct an alternative."
- "I think there's a deep metaphor doing all the work here - something like 'growth is health' that nobody is examining. Surface the official account, the system, the worldview, and the myth, then deliberately change the metaphor and rebuild a preferred future from it."
- "Give me a four-layer read of this contested issue - litany, system, worldview, myth - for the current 'used future,' and then a reconstructed preferred future per layer anchored by a changed deep metaphor. Whose framing is privileged here?"
- "We keep optimizing this and it keeps feeling wrong, because we've never questioned the civilizational story we're inside. Deconstruct the framing as competing readings, then reconstruct an alternative future, not a forecast."
- "Help me reframe this long-horizon, value-laden issue by reading it at depth and rewriting the guiding metaphor - I want alternative futures opened up, not the current one tuned."

## Should NOT trigger (wrong tool / near-miss)

- "This problem keeps recurring despite our fixes - take it down through event, pattern, structure, and mental models and tell me *the* root structure and where to intervene." (a single-cause descent to one diagnosis and a leverage point is `think-iceberg-model`; CLA refuses a single true cause and rebuilds upward into alternative futures instead of pairing levels with interventions.)
- "Build me a few alternative external futures we don't control, organized by the two biggest uncertainties, and tell me which moves survive all of them." (modeling uncontrollable external worlds in a 2x2 is `think-scenario-planning`; CLA deconstructs the narrative and myth layer of *one* issue, it does not build a set of external worlds.)
- "We're launching the self-serve free tier - trace the first, second, and third-order consequences of that one move radiating outward." (one consequence map from one decision is `think-futures-wheel`, not a four-layer descent-and-rebuild of an issue's deep story.)
- "Give me one fresh way of looking at this whole problem - a single new frame that reframes everything." (abducing one new standpoint for the whole problem is `think-frame-creation`; CLA rewrites four stacked layers at once and changes a civilizational myth, which a single reframe does not do.)
- "Who did we leave out when we drew the boundary of this problem, and what's inside versus outside the system we're improving?" (interrogating where the problem's boundary is drawn and who is excluded is `think-boundary-critique`; CLA may surface whose worldview is privileged but its apparatus is the four-layer descent-and-rebuild, not a boundary judgment.)
- "Assume our platform bet has already failed two years from now - work backward to everything that could have caused it." (imagining one specified failure and reasoning to its causes is `think-premortem`; CLA is not failure-anchored and is not a forecast.)

## Output checks (a good output must)

- [ ] Confirm the issue is contested, value-laden, long-horizon, and *framing-stuck* - not a simple, technical, or single-cause problem.
- [ ] Read all four layers for the current "used future": **litany, system, worldview, myth/metaphor**, each distinct from the one above (the myth is a metaphor, not a restated system claim).
- [ ] At the worldview layer, name *whose* worldview is privileged and surface at least one competing reading - not a single authoritative cause.
- [ ] **Change the deep metaphor deliberately** and actually perform the reconstruction: a transformed worldview, a reconstructed system, and a reconstructed litany that all follow from the new metaphor. (A descent with no reconstructed myth is not CLA.)
- [ ] Frame the worldview and myth readings as interpretive and contestable - not "the real myth" presented as discovered fact.
- [ ] Stop at the reframe: do not output a forecast, a ranking, or a decision, and do not manufacture profundity where the issue does not warrant it.
- [ ] Deliver the four-layer matrix artifact (current beside reconstructed, with the changed metaphor named as the anchor), not prose.
- [ ] Not overclaim: keep to a reframing aid; the evidence is conceptually-plausible-but-untested and transferred, with no measured outcome effect.

## Value vs unaided baseline

Asked the same question, a strong model tends to argue the issue at the litany and system levels - restating the metrics and the mechanics - and, if pushed "deeper," to name a single root cause or a single better framing and stop there, treating its reading as the truth. It rarely descends all the way to an unconscious civilizational *metaphor*, almost never holds *competing* worldview readings instead of converging on one, and - decisively - does not then **rebuild upward**: change the deep metaphor on purpose and propagate a transformed worldview, system, and litany into a coherent preferred future. This skill forces that discipline: a genuine four-layer descent past litany and system into worldview and myth, the poststructuralist move of asking whose framing this is and what it hides, a deliberately changed anchor metaphor, and the full upward reconstruction a forward-only model skips - delivered as a current-versus-preferred matrix with the deep readings marked as contestable. It converts an exhausted surface argument into a reframed alternative future, while refusing to pose as a forecast or a decision.
