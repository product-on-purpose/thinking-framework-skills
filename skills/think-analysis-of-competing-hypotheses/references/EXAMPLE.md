# ACH - Honest Redirect Brief - Worked Example

A completed run of `think-analysis-of-competing-hypotheses`, on the shared Northwind scenario. This is the quality bar: the caveat leads, no matrix is produced as valid, and the brief routes to the move the job actually needs.

> Northwind is a B2B SaaS weighing a self-serve free-tier launch and feeling stuck on "build it or not."

> **Controlled-evidence caveat (read first):** ACH was tested and found wanting. It raised confidence with no accuracy gain (Otzipka 2025) and did not reduce confirmation bias in randomized trials (Dhami et al. 2019; Karvetski and Mandel 2020). This brief does not build the matrix as if valid.

---

## What was asked

- "Use ACH to figure out why trial-to-paid conversion dropped this quarter: is it pricing, onboarding, or a worse-fit lead mix?" The real decision: where to spend the next two sprints to recover conversion.

## Why not the ACH matrix

- The controlled record on ACH is null-to-negative on its own move and purpose, and it tends to raise confidence without improving accuracy. Worse here, scoring "evidence" against three hypotheses would treat correlated signals (a pricing change and a lead-mix change that happened the same month) as independent and equally weighted, manufacturing a confident "least-inconsistent" answer from entangled data.

## The evidence-based move instead

- Attack the leading thesis to find where it breaks: `think-red-team-light`.
- Separate evidence from inference in the claim set: `think-evidence-vs-inference-sort`.
- Test a named option's load-bearing conditions: `think-what-would-have-to-be-true`.

- **Chosen move:** `think-evidence-vs-inference-sort` first, because the three hypotheses are tangled with assumptions ("conversion dropped because of pricing") that are actually inferences, not measured facts. Sorting evidence from inference will show which of the three even has data behind it. Then `think-red-team-light` on whichever explanation survives, to find where it fails before committing two sprints.

## If you still need rival-hypothesis discrimination

- The better-grounded move is per-item necessity/sufficiency testing within this one case (process tracing): for each candidate cause, ask what evidence would be necessary or sufficient if it were the driver, and check the actual record against that, rather than tallying inconsistencies across a matrix. Do not present a matrix verdict as valid.

---

*Note: ACH was requested by name, so a flat refusal would not help. The honest answer leads with the controlled record, declines to manufacture a confident matrix from entangled signals, and hands the user a concrete, better-grounded next move.*
