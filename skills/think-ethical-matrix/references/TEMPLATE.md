# Ethical Matrix - Template

Fill this in. The deliverable is the ethical matrix - affected-party rows (voiceless ones marked) by principle columns, every cell a tagged impact specification, plus the trade-off pattern read-out and the no-verdict footer - not a prose essay.

> The matrix maps the moral terrain; it does NOT weigh it. Do not present the filled grid as a score, a ranking, or a recommendation. The payoff is the trade-off pattern read-out, not a tidy completed grid.

## No-verdict footer (required)

> This matrix maps the moral terrain of the option across affected parties and principles. It is **not a score, a ranking, or a recommendation**, and it emits **no verdict**. The grid surfaces the trade-offs and the contested cells; the weighing of them is left to deliberation among the people who must decide (Schroeder and Palmer, 2003: the matrix is helpful for unpacking and fact-finding but "much less helpful" for weighing).

## Evidence caveat (required - ships with the artifact by construction)

> **Evidence tier: P (practitioner).** The ethical matrix has roughly twenty-five years of multi-domain application and serious methodological scrutiny, but **no controlled outcome study** exists - there is no measured evidence that using it produces better or more defensible ethical assessments, and no effect size is claimed here. All of that evidence is **human group-deliberation practice; none is on AI agents**, so an agent-produced matrix is a transferred-evidence application, not a validated one. Treat this artifact as a trade-off-mapping aid that makes the trade-offs visible and contestable - not as a measure of how ethical the option is, and not as a decision. See `evidence/dossier.md`.
