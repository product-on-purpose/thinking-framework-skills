# Rival-Explanation Evidence Ledger - Template

Fill this in. The deliverable is the ledger - the rivals, their mechanism chains, the per-item diagnosticity typing, the surviving explanation with residual uncertainty, and the most decisive missing observation - not a prose essay and not a tally.

> Evidence is weighed by diagnosticity, not by count. One decisive observation (a failed hoop, a smoking gun, a doubly-decisive find) outranks any amount of straw-in-the-wind support. Do NOT pick the explanation with the most supporting items. Do NOT assign a test type after seeing the evidence - the expected fingerprints are stated first. If nothing available is diagnostic, the honest answer is "non-diagnostic," not a manufactured winner.

Test-type key:
- **Hoop** (certain, not unique): failing it eliminates the rival; passing keeps it alive without confirming.
- **Smoking gun** (unique, not certain): finding it strongly confirms; not finding only mildly weakens.
- **Straw in the wind** (neither): a weak nudge, never decisive.
- **Doubly decisive** (both): confirms one rival and eliminates the others.

## Evidence caveat (ships with the artifact - do not delete)

> This ledger is a structured single-case adjudication aid, not a measured accuracy improver. The method's governing evidence tier is **P (practitioner)**: process tracing has a deep, peer-reviewed methodological literature on inferential validity in case-study research, but there is no controlled trial showing that using it improves reasoning accuracy, for humans or for agents. The evidence is **transferred from human case-study methodology and is not agent-validated**. The only nearby controlled evidence is negative and belongs to the cousin method ACH; it sets no tier here. Treat this ledger as a disciplined way to discriminate rival explanations and to name the next decisive observation - not as proof that the surviving explanation is correct. When the evidence is all non-diagnostic, "non-diagnostic" is the honest result. See `evidence/dossier.md`.
