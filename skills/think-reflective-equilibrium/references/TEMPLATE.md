# Reflective Equilibrium - Coherence Set + Revision Ledger - Template

> **Evidence caveat (read first):** Reflective equilibrium is tier C: philosophically central since Rawls (1971) but empirically untested as a procedure. "Equilibrium" has no external stopping test, so it cannot be distinguished from "I stopped looking"; the inputs can be a reshuffling of prejudices (Brandt 1979); and the method can license rationalizing a preferred outcome. Even idealized, convergence is rare (Freivogel 2023, ~27 percent). The value below is the revision ledger, not the declaration of coherence. Do not declare global coherence where conflicts remain, and do not invent effectiveness numbers.
