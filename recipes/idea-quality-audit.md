# Recipe: idea-quality-audit

**Job:** decide which of a batch of candidate ideas are worth committing to, by scoring them on explicit quality dimensions and then stress-testing the strongest few.

**Use when:** you have a pile of candidate ideas (from a brainstorm, a backlog, or an ideation skill) and need to separate the genuinely strong from the merely fluent before investing in any of them. It pairs naturally after an ideation skill (`think-brainwriting`, `think-scamper`) that generated the batch.

## Chain

1. **`think-decision-option-review`** (`skills/think-decision-option-review/SKILL.md`)
   - Treat the ideas as options; score them against weighted idea-quality criteria (for example novelty, feasibility, value, specificity); surface the tradeoffs and a ranked shortlist.
   - Carry forward: the **top few** ideas, and where the scoring was soft.
2. **`think-red-team-light`** (`skills/think-red-team-light/SKILL.md`)
   - Adversarially attack the top-ranked ideas - the strongest objections, steelmanned - to find which survive scrutiny and which only sounded good.
   - Carry forward: the **survivors and their open weaknesses**.

## Composite artifact

An idea-quality audit: a scored, ranked shortlist of the batch, with the strongest few stress-tested, and a verdict on which ideas are worth committing to. The point is that commitment goes to ideas that survive scrutiny, not to the ones that read best on first pass.

## Why a recipe, not a skill

"Idea-quality audit" has no separable cognitive mechanism of its own: scoring a batch is `think-decision-option-review` (options become ideas), and pressure-testing is `think-red-team-light`. It ships as this thin chain rather than a near-twin skill (the 2026-06-03 catalog vetting reached this verdict). Use `think-decision-option-review` on its own for a one-off scored comparison; reach for this recipe when you specifically want score-then-stress-test over a batch of ideas.

## Token discipline

Carry only the top-ranked shortlist between steps (the ranked few plus their soft spots, then the red-team survivors), not the full scoring matrix for every idea.
