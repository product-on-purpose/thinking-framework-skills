## Components

### Skills (67)

- [`think-abstraction-laddering`](skills/think-abstraction-laddering/) - Builds an abstraction ladder that moves a problem up (why) and down (how) and marks the rung to work at. Use when a problem arrives as a bare solution or vague aspiration, or people argue at different levels.
- [`think-affinity-mapping`](skills/think-affinity-mapping/) - Produces a clustered theme map that groups many raw notes, quotes or data points bottom-up into named, traceable themes. Use when dozens to hundreds of items, such as research notes or tickets, need emergent structure.
- [`think-after-action-review`](skills/think-after-action-review/) - Produces an after-action review that compares what was expected with what happened, diagnoses the gaps, and assigns owned sustain-and-change actions. Use when a project, launch, sprint or incident has finished.
- [`think-analysis-of-competing-hypotheses`](skills/think-analysis-of-competing-hypotheses/) - Handles a by-name request for ACH (Analysis of Competing Hypotheses), the evidence-by-hypothesis disconfirmation matrix, honestly. Controlled trials found ACH raises confidence with no accuracy gain and does not reduce confirmation bias, so this skill does NOT build the matrix as if valid. It leads with that evidence, then routes to the evidence-based move the job actually needs (think-red-team-light, think-evidence-vs-inference-sort, or think-what-would-have-to-be-true). Use only when someone asks for ACH or a competing-hypotheses matrix by name.
- [`think-argument-mapping`](skills/think-argument-mapping/) - Produces an argument map of a claim's reasons, hidden co-premises and objections, flagging the weakest links. Use when an argument must be checked for soundness, or a fluent case may hide a broken inference.
- [`think-assumption-reversal`](skills/think-assumption-reversal/) - Generates non-obvious ideas by negating the assumptions a problem or solution rests on and reframing from each reversal. Use when options feel stuck in default constraints or premises go unquestioned.
- [`think-authentic-dissent`](skills/think-authentic-dissent/) - Checks whether a decision has genuine dissent or only surface consensus, and plans how to elicit and protect real dissent, flagging constructed views. Use when consensus feels too easy or a high-stakes call needs real challenge.
- [`think-backcasting`](skills/think-backcasting/) - Produces a backcast path that fixes a desired future and reasons backward through milestones to the next step available now. Use when a long-horizon or transformative goal is anchored too low by forward planning.
- [`think-belief-update-routine`](skills/think-belief-update-routine/) - Produces a belief-update ledger that re-scores standing beliefs against new evidence on a cadence, sizing and justifying each change. Use when open beliefs, forecasts or assumptions should keep tracking evidence.
- [`think-boundary-critique`](skills/think-boundary-critique/) - Audits boundary judgments in a frame (who benefits, decides, counts as expert and has standing) and names who is excluded. Use when a plan, metric or improvement claim decides whose interests count, or in contested multi-party cases.
- [`think-brainwriting`](skills/think-brainwriting/) - Generates ideas as silent brainwriting does, in independent streams that build on each other without anchoring on the first voice, consolidated into an idea pool. Use when breadth matters and anchoring is a risk.
- [`think-causal-layered-analysis`](skills/think-causal-layered-analysis/) - Produces a four-layer matrix (litany, system, worldview, myth) of the current future beside a preferred one, anchored by a new deep metaphor. Use when an issue is stuck because its framing and worldviews are stuck.
- [`think-causal-loop-diagrams`](skills/think-causal-loop-diagrams/) - Builds a signed causal loop diagram, labels each loop reinforcing or balancing, and reads the dynamics from the dominant loop. Use when a situation feeds back on itself and keeps accelerating, stalling or overshooting.
- [`think-complexity-domain-sort`](skills/think-complexity-domain-sort/) - Runs a Complexity Domain Sort, sorting a situation into Clear, Complicated, Complex, Chaotic, or Confusion and choosing the response posture that fits, caveat-first. It leads with the weak evidence (the 2021 PMC internal-medicine review found scientific proof of its validity has yet to be provided), then forces the discipline the bare label lacks, namely an explicit response posture and a concrete next action rather than just a name. Use only when this complexity sort (also known as Cynefin) is asked for by name; for cause decomposition prefer think-issue-tree.
- [`think-concept-mapping`](skills/think-concept-mapping/) - Builds a concept map of nodes joined by labeled links that read as propositions, with cross-links, then surfaces gaps and doubtful propositions. Use when many interrelated concepts need every relationship named.
- [`think-consider-the-unknowns`](skills/think-consider-the-unknowns/) - Produces a known-unknowns ledger of the missing evidence and unknown variables behind a judgment, rated by bearing and obtainability, then re-rates confidence. Use when a one-off judgment rests on thin evidence.
- [`think-contradiction-resolution`](skills/think-contradiction-resolution/) - Converts a stuck trade-off into a contradiction to dissolve by separating the requirements in time, space, scale or condition, or flags it as genuine. Use when a decision has hardened into an either/or like speed versus quality.
- [`think-contradiction-tension-mapping`](skills/think-contradiction-tension-mapping/) - Produces a both/and polarity map of two interdependent poles, with each pole's upsides and downsides, warning signs and actions. Use when a group keeps swinging between two true opposites that must be managed, not solved.
- [`think-decision-journal`](skills/think-decision-journal/) - Produces a decision journal entry that records a decision, its rationale, predicted outcome, confidence and assumptions when it is made, for honest later review. Use when committing to a consequential, uncertain decision.
- [`think-decision-option-review`](skills/think-decision-option-review/) - Produces a criteria-weighted option matrix by comparing a set of options against weighted criteria, scoring each, surfacing the explicit tradeoffs, and recommending one while flagging where the scoring is soft. Use when choosing among several real options, or when a decision needs its tradeoffs made explicit rather than left to intuition.
- [`think-dialectical-bootstrapping`](skills/think-dialectical-bootstrapping/) - Produces a dialectical estimate by making a first estimate, assuming it is wrong, making a second, and averaging the two. Use when a hard one-off numeric estimate must be committed with no second judge, base rate or better data.
- [`think-eisenhower-moscow-pareto`](skills/think-eisenhower-moscow-pareto/) - Runs one of the three lightweight prioritization presets (Eisenhower urgent-important 2x2, MoSCoW Must/Should/Could/Wont bucketing, or Pareto vital-few cut) caveat-first. It leads with the fact that this is a bundle of three different operations stapled by the word "prioritization", not one move, and each is weakly evidenced, then produces the one preset the user named with the discipline a canned template lacks. Use only when an Eisenhower matrix, MoSCoW list, or Pareto chart is asked for by name; for the rigorous versions prefer think-theory-of-constraints, think-one-way-vs-two-way-door, or think-decision-option-review.
- [`think-ethical-matrix`](skills/think-ethical-matrix/) - Produces an ethical matrix crossing affected parties, voiceless ones included, against principles such as wellbeing, autonomy and fairness, read as a trade-off pattern. Use when a proposal poses a moral trade-off among several parties.
- [`think-evidence-vs-inference-sort`](skills/think-evidence-vs-inference-sort/) - Produces an evidence/inference ledger that sorts the claims in a document or conclusion into evidence, inference and assumption, rates each inference and flags uncited claims. Use when a conclusion must be trusted or audited.
- [`think-expected-value-decision-tree`](skills/think-expected-value-decision-tree/) - Evaluates actions under uncertainty with a decision tree of choice and chance nodes, explicit probabilities, an expected value per option and what would flip it. Use when uncertain outcomes can be given rough probabilities.
- [`think-far-analogy-ideation`](skills/think-far-analogy-ideation/) - Generates novel solutions by stating a problem's deep structure, mapping it to distant domains such as nature or other industries, and transferring the mechanism, not the surface. Use when near, obvious solutions are exhausted.
- [`think-fermi-estimation`](skills/think-fermi-estimation/) - Produces a Fermi worksheet that builds an unknown quantity from order-of-magnitude factors into a point estimate and a compounded range. Use when a number is needed and no lookup data or reference class exists, as in market sizing.
- [`think-five-whys`](skills/think-five-whys/) - Runs a Five Whys root-cause trace caveat-first. It leads with the weak evidence (Card 2017 found the single-chain method oversimplifies multi-causal problems), then forces the discipline a bare why-chain lacks, namely flagging at each step whether more than one cause could apply. Use only when Five Whys is asked for by name; for any problem that might have more than one cause prefer think-issue-tree.
- [`think-frame-creation`](skills/think-frame-creation/) - Generates a new problem frame from theme analysis of the wider context, treating the problem as if it were something else, and the solutions that opens. Use when a paradoxical problem resists solutions in its original frame.
- [`think-framework-advisor`](skills/think-framework-advisor/) - Produces a prioritized, evidence-graded Thinking Plan that diagnoses which thinking frameworks a situation actually needs - the dominant cognitive job, a short sequence of the fewest fitting frameworks to apply (each with its evidence tier, expected artifact, and a ready-to-run prompt), and what not to use. Recommends only frameworks this library ships. Use when unsure which thinking method or skill fits a decision, problem, or stuck point, when overwhelmed by where to start, or when you need a recommended plan of which frameworks to use and why rather than running one yourself.
- [`think-futures-wheel`](skills/think-futures-wheel/) - Produces a consequence map by tracing the first, second, and third order effects of a change or decision radiating outward from the center, surfacing ripples beyond the obvious and flagging the high-impact branches. Use when a decision has knock-on effects over time, or when first-order thinking is missing downstream risks and opportunities.
- [`think-iceberg-model`](skills/think-iceberg-model/) - Produces an iceberg that traces a problem down from the visible event to patterns, structures and mental models, with the intervention each level implies. Use when a problem keeps recurring despite event-level fixes.
- [`think-interest-based-negotiation`](skills/think-interest-based-negotiation/) - Produces a negotiation preparation map that separates positions from interests, values the best alternative and agreement zone, and invents options for mutual gain. Use when another party must agree on more than one issue.
- [`think-interval-calibration-check`](skills/think-interval-calibration-check/) - Audits whether a human-stated confidence interval means what it claims, with an equivalent-bet test and a hit-rate score, producing corrected intervals and a scorecard. Use when an unaudited interval drives a consequential plan.
- [`think-issue-tree`](skills/think-issue-tree/) - Produces an issue tree that decomposes one big, ambiguous question top-down into a mutually-exclusive, collectively-exhaustive (MECE) set of smaller sub-questions, branch by branch, until the leaves are small enough to answer with data or judgment. Use when a question is too broad or multi-cause to answer as posed (for example "why is churn rising?", "where is margin leaking?", "should we launch a free tier?"), when analysis work must be split into non-overlapping parts, or when coverage matters and missing a whole category would be costly. Not for evaluating a given argument's soundness (use argument-mapping) or clustering existing notes (use affinity-mapping).
- [`think-ladder-of-inference-check`](skills/think-ladder-of-inference-check/) - Produces a reasoning trace of how a conclusion climbed from observable data through selected data and added meaning, flagging the riskiest leap. Use when a conclusion feels certain but rests on interpretation, or is contested.
- [`think-linear-model-aggregation`](skills/think-linear-model-aggregation/) - Builds a simple mechanical scoring model, a few weighted cues combined by a fixed formula, for a repeated predictive judgment. Use when the same judgment recurs, such as screening or triage, and gut calls are inconsistent.
- [`think-minimax-regret`](skills/think-minimax-regret/) - Produces a regret matrix with the minimax pick and the binding worst-case state. Use when a few discrete options face a few uncontrollable states and no defensible probabilities exist.
- [`think-morphological-analysis`](skills/think-morphological-analysis/) - Produces a morphological field of parameters and their values, pruned by cross-consistency into coherent configurations. Use when a solution is a configuration across several dimensions and one default combination would win.
- [`think-natural-frequency-bayesian`](skills/think-natural-frequency-bayesian/) - Converts a conditional-probability or base-rate question into natural frequencies over a concrete population to compute the real posterior, given real input rates. Use when interpreting a test result or other positive signal.
- [`think-one-way-vs-two-way-door`](skills/think-one-way-vs-two-way-door/) - Produces a reversibility classification that labels a decision a two-way or one-way door before any analysis, and matches deliberation and sign-off to it. Use when it is unclear how much process a decision deserves.
- [`think-pairwise-comparison`](skills/think-pairwise-comparison/) - Produces a pairwise comparison matrix of binary votes, a derived ranking and a consistency check, with no criteria or absolute scores. Use when items must be ranked but no defensible scale or articulable criteria exist.
- [`think-parallel-perspectives-review`](skills/think-parallel-perspectives-review/) - Evaluates a decision or idea through several deliberately separated lenses in turn (facts, upside, risks, intuition, alternatives, process) so that no single mode dominates, then synthesizes them into a balanced read, producing a multi-lens review. Use when a choice needs a rounded look, or when risk-aversion or optimism is drowning out the other perspectives.
- [`think-premortem`](skills/think-premortem/) - Generates a ranked risk register that stress-tests a planned decision by imagining it has already failed, surfacing the likely causes and pairing each with a mitigation, tripwire, and kill criterion. Use when about to commit to a launch, hire, investment, migration, or any risky, hard-to-reverse decision, or when you need risks surfaced before committing.
- [`think-problem-restatement`](skills/think-problem-restatement/) - Generates several genuinely different framings of an ambiguous problem by varying altitude, stakeholder, and goal-versus-implementation, then selects the most useful one to solve and produces a reframed problem statement with How Might We angles. Use when a problem is vague, arrived as a symptom or a pre-baked solution, or before committing significant work to solving the wrong thing.
- [`think-process-tracing`](skills/think-process-tracing/) - Evaluates rival explanations of one case by typing each piece of evidence by diagnosticity (hoop, smoking-gun and other tests) and eliminating rivals. Use when one outcome has rival explanations and mechanism-level evidence.
- [`think-pyramid-principle`](skills/think-pyramid-principle/) - Produces a pyramid that structures a recommendation answer-first, with one governing thought over ordered MECE arguments and evidence. Use when an existing conclusion must reach a busy reader, or a write-up buries its point.
- [`think-qualitative-comparative-analysis`](skills/think-qualitative-comparative-analysis/) - Handles a by-name request for QCA (Qualitative Comparative Analysis), the truth-table-and-Boolean-minimization method, honestly. At session scale the input almost never exists and the method certifies configurations from noise, so this skill does NOT build the truth table as if valid. It leads with that evidence, then routes to the evidence-based move the job actually needs (think-reference-class-forecasting for a set of comparable cases, or think-process-tracing for a single case). Use only when someone asks for QCA or a truth-table minimization by name.
- [`think-question-burst`](skills/think-question-burst/) - Generates a rapid burst of questions about a problem, with no answers, then ranks them and picks the most catalytic one. Use when stuck, too attached to one framing, or in need of a better question.
- [`think-random-frameworks`](skills/think-random-frameworks/) - Generates three off-pattern framework analyses of a topic by drawing frameworks at random, applying each regardless of fit, and harvesting the non-obvious angles the unexpected lenses expose. Use when a framing feels frozen or fixated and the goal is to break out of it with deliberately unranked methods rather than the obvious best-fit ones. Use when relevance-ranked selection would only reinforce the stuck view and a random draw is wanted to surface what a fitted analysis would miss.
- [`think-red-team-light`](skills/think-red-team-light/) - Produces an adversarial critique by constructing the strongest case against a proposal or thesis (the best objections an intelligent adversary would raise), then judging which objections actually land and what would rebut them. Use when a plan has too-easy consensus and needs pressure-testing, or to steelman the opposition before committing.
- [`think-reference-class-forecasting`](skills/think-reference-class-forecasting/) - Produces a reference-class estimate by defining a class of similar past cases, taking their base-rate distribution of outcomes, and anchoring the forecast on that outside view rather than the optimistic inside view, with a conservative adjustment for specifics. Use when forecasting cost, time, or odds of success for a project prone to optimism or the planning fallacy.
- [`think-reflective-equilibrium`](skills/think-reflective-equilibrium/) - Runs reflective equilibrium (mutual adjustment between considered case judgments, general principles, and background theories) caveat-first. It leads with the weak evidence (philosophically central since Rawls 1971 but empirically untested as a procedure, with three failure modes that bite in a bounded session), then forces the discipline a bare run lacks, namely an explicit revision ledger that records which commitment gave way and why. Use only when reflective equilibrium is asked for by name; for impartial allocation prefer think-veil-of-ignorance-reasoning, for a parties-by-principles audit think-ethical-matrix.
- [`think-research-framework`](skills/think-research-framework/) - Researches a thinking framework end to end and produces a schema-valid proposed registry entry plus a learning dossier, grading the evidence on the seven-tier model and assessing overlap against the shipped catalog. Use when you need an honest evidence grade and a build-or-fold verdict for a candidate method before turning it into a skill, when reproducing the evidence-and-overlap vetting on a method the catalog already judged, or when you need a ranked shortlist of new candidate methods in a family.
- [`think-role-storming`](skills/think-role-storming/) - Produces a persona-tagged idea list, generated while inhabiting a chosen non-self identity to lower self-censorship. Use when idea flow is throttled by self-consciousness or a fixated frame and stranger candidates are wanted.
- [`think-scamper`](skills/think-scamper/) - Generates variations on an existing idea, product or process through seven prompts (substitute, combine, adapt, modify, put to other use, eliminate, reverse), then shortlists. Use when a seed idea needs to get past the obvious.
- [`think-scenario-planning`](skills/think-scenario-planning/) - Produces a scenario set, 2-4 contrasting and internally consistent short narratives of alternative external futures named by two critical-uncertainty axes, plus a robustness read of the strategy across the worlds (which moves survive every world, which early signals show which world is arriving, which options to keep open). Use when the planning horizon is long and the decisive forces are outside control and not reliably predictable, and a single implicit forecast is driving the strategy. Not forecasting and not a single preferred path.
- [`think-speculative-harms-anti-goals`](skills/think-speculative-harms-anti-goals/) - Produces an anti-goals register by imagining a design's success at scale harming third parties or exploited by bad actors, then binding each harm into requirements. Use when a feature's success could harm people beyond its customers.
- [`think-stocks-and-flows-reasoning`](skills/think-stocks-and-flows-reasoning/) - Produces a stock-flow map that separates an accumulating quantity from the inflows and outflows that change it, reasoning from the net flow. Use when an accumulation such as cash, backlog or debt may be moving against intuition.
- [`think-swot`](skills/think-swot/) - Runs a SWOT analysis (strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats) caveat-first. It leads with the weak evidence (Hill and Westbrook 1997 found real SWOT grids did not amount to analysis), then forces the discipline a bare grid lacks, namely evidence-tagged and prioritized factors plus a TOWS matching step that turns the four lists into strategic options. Use only when a SWOT or TOWS is asked for by name; for genuine situation decomposition prefer think-issue-tree.
- [`think-theory-of-constraints`](skills/think-theory-of-constraints/) - Produces a constraint-intervention plan that confirms the one step capping a system's throughput by capacity against demand, then sets its exploit, subordinate and elevate decisions. Use when a flow's output is capped by one step.
- [`think-three-horizons`](skills/think-three-horizons/) - Produces a three horizons map (declining present, contested middle, emerging future) that locates the actor in it. Use when a group stuck in a present-versus-future binary must manage decline and grow the new at once.
- [`think-top3`](skills/think-top3/) - Produces a worked three-framework analysis of a topic. Ranks the library's thinking frameworks for relevance, applies the top three so each emits its real artifact, and reconciles them into one cross-framework synthesis. Use when a decision, problem, or stuck point deserves more than one lens and the goal is worked output now rather than a plan of which methods to run later. Use when several frameworks plausibly fit and the most relevant few should be ranked, executed, and integrated into a single read.
- [`think-veil-of-ignorance-reasoning`](skills/think-veil-of-ignorance-reasoning/) - Produces a veiled-decision comparison that decides a values trade-off as if the decider could be any affected party, naming the gap self-interest drove. Use when a decision splits benefit and burden and the decider is one party.
- [`think-walton-argumentation-schemes`](skills/think-walton-argumentation-schemes/) - Evaluates one presumptive argument by naming its scheme, such as expert opinion, analogy or slippery slope, exposing implicit premises and asking that scheme's critical questions. Use when one argument's presumption needs testing.
- [`think-what-would-have-to-be-true`](skills/think-what-would-have-to-be-true/) - Converts a strategy, option or contested claim into the conditions that would have to be true for it to be best, rates each, and picks which to test first. Use when evaluating a strategic bet or a clash of opinions.
- [`think-woop`](skills/think-woop/) - Produces a WOOP card (Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, Plan) that contrasts the outcome with the main internal obstacle and binds an if-then plan to it. Use when a goal is already chosen but follow-through keeps failing.

### Subagents (3, Claude-only)

- [`think-audit-reasoning`](agents/think-audit-reasoning.md) - Check whether a conclusion is sound before trusting it, by sorting evidence from inference, reconstructing the inferential climb, and reviewing through separated lenses. Runs the 3-skill think-audit-reasoning chain in its own context and returns only the finished reasoning audit, so the intermediate artifacts never enter the caller's conversation. Use when the caller wants the reasoning audit rather than the working. Do NOT use when the user wants to see or steer each step (run the /think-audit-reasoning command inline instead), or for a job one method answers (use that skill).
- [`think-research-framework`](agents/think-research-framework.md) - Researches a named thinking method end to end and returns a verdict a human can act on - grades its evidence conservatively on the seven-tier model, proves or disproves distinctness against the shipped catalog, drafts its learning dossier, and emits a schema-valid proposed registry entry. Returns BUILD or FOLD, and folding is a success. Use when the question is whether a method deserves to ship, when an existing catalog judgment needs reproducing against current evidence, or when a family needs uncovered candidates shortlisted. Do NOT use to author a skill whose verdict is already settled, to run a framework on the user's own problem (use that skill, or think-framework-advisor), or for a product or domain question rather than an evaluation of a thinking method. Never writes the registry; a human admits the entry.
- [`think-stress-test-decision`](agents/think-stress-test-decision.md) - Pressure-test a consequential decision before committing, by comparing options, surfacing the conditions that must hold, imagining failure, and sanity-checking the estimate against base rates. Runs the 4-skill think-stress-test-decision chain in its own context and returns only the finished decision brief, so the intermediate artifacts never enter the caller's conversation. Use when the caller wants the decision brief rather than the working. Do NOT use when the user wants to see or steer each step (run the /think-stress-test-decision command inline instead), or for a job one method answers (use that skill).

### Commands (10)

- [`/think-audit-reasoning`](commands/think-audit-reasoning.md) - Check whether a conclusion is sound before trusting it, by sorting evidence from inference, reconstructing the inferential climb, and reviewing through separated lenses.

- [`/think-kepner-tregoe`](commands/think-kepner-tregoe.md) - Run the Kepner-Tregoe rational process as a chain of shipped moves, taking a bounded operational problem through cause-finding, then a weighted choice among options, then a forward risk pass, so a fix is de-risked end to end.
- [`/think-pdca-a3`](commands/think-pdca-a3.md) - Run the Plan-Do-Check-Act improvement loop as a chain of shipped moves, taking a performance gap through root-cause and countermeasure choice, then a structured review of actual versus expected, then a standardize-or-iterate decision.
- [`/think-reframe-problem`](commands/think-reframe-problem.md) - Make sure you are solving the right problem before generating solutions, by restating it, sorting evidence from inference, and reviewing it through separated lenses.
- [`/think-research-framework`](commands/think-research-framework.md) - Research a thinking framework and produce its evidence-graded dossier plus a schema-valid proposed registry entry, or discover candidate methods in a family. Use to vet a method before building it into a skill, reproduce the evidence-and-overlap vetting, or shortlist new candidates.
- [`/think-stress-test-decision`](commands/think-stress-test-decision.md) - Pressure-test a consequential decision before committing, by comparing options, surfacing the conditions that must hold, imagining failure, and sanity-checking the estimate against base rates.
