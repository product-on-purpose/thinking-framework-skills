The fastest way to know whether this is for you is to watch it work. Below, three people take real, hard decisions through the frameworks - the exact prompt they typed, and the full artifact it produced. No teasers; the whole output is on the page.

The cast spans the library's range on purpose: an individual deciding fast, a team decision with technical consequences, and a deliberation with ethical stakes run by hand.

## Browse by person

### Mira, startup founder (casual prompts)

Recurring high-stakes calls under uncertainty, usually solo, usually fast.

- [Where do I even start?](./mira-advisor-first/) - she describes the mess to the advisor and gets a Thinking Plan.
- [Reframe a hiring decision](./mira-hiring-reframe/) - "who do I hire" becomes "what capability are we missing."
- [Premortem a launch](./mira-launch-premortem/) - a one-line prompt yields a ranked risk register with tripwires and kill criteria.
- [Choose between two pricing models](./mira-pricing-decision/) - the full stress-test-a-decision recipe: three chained artifacts.
- [Stress-test a pivot](./mira-pivot-stress-test/) - what would have to be true, sorted by what she can test cheaply.

### Daniel, senior engineer (organized prompts)

Technical and architectural decisions with cross-team consequences.

- [Trace a recurring bug](./daniel-recurring-bug/) - rival causes adjudicated by the diagnostic weight of the evidence.
- [Map an underperforming system](./daniel-system-loop/) - the feedback loops behind a queue that keeps growing.
- [Challenge a load-bearing assumption](./daniel-assumption/) - the inference chain from raw data to "rip out the cache."
- [Choose between three architectures](./daniel-architecture-choice/) - a weighted scoring table with an honest sensitivity note.
- [Map a migration's consequences](./daniel-migration-consequences/) - first, second, and third-order effects.
- [Audit the reasoning in a proposal](./daniel-audit-reasoning-recipe/) - the audit-reasoning recipe: evidence-vs-inference, then an argument map.

### Priya, policy analyst (detailed prompts, run by hand)

Decisions with ethical dimensions and public accountability, often worked on paper.

- [Map stakeholders before a policy](./priya-ethical-matrix/) - the ethical matrix, filled in by hand.
- [Deliberate a values trade-off by hand](./priya-veil-of-ignorance/) - the clearest "you do not need an agent" demonstration.
- [Audit the argument in a proposal](./priya-argument-mapping/) - the load-bearing premise made explicit.
- [Record a decision before a public commitment](./priya-decision-journal/) - a journal entry built for later calibration.
- [Work a contested docket](./priya-ipa-recipe/) - the issue-position-argument recipe across many conflicting comments.
