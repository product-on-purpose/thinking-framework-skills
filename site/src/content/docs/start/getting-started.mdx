This is a library of **thinking frameworks rebuilt as skills** an AI agent can run, and that humans can follow by hand. Each one takes a messy situation and produces a concrete artifact, not a wall of prose: a ranked risk register, a weighted option matrix, an argument map, an honest base-rate estimate.

## Your first framework: a premortem

A [premortem](../../frameworks/think-premortem/) <Badge text="Evidence: S/M" variant="success" /> stress-tests a plan by imagining it has *already failed* and working backward to why, then turning each cause into a tripwire and a pre-decided response.

<Steps>

1. **Pick a real decision** you are about to commit to: a launch, a hire, a migration.

2. **Run the skill** in Claude Code (or follow its numbered steps by hand with a pen):

   ```bash
   /think-premortem "we're about to launch a free tier to drive signups"
   ```

3. **Get a ranked risk register.** For each top risk: a leading signal, a mitigation, an owner, and a kill criterion.

</Steps>

### What a good result looks like

So you can tell a real run from a vague one, here is the shape of the output, truncated from the [full worked example](/frameworks/think-premortem/#deep-dive-worked-example):

> **Decision:** Launch a self-serve free tier in 6 weeks to accelerate top-of-funnel growth.
> **Horizon:** 6 months after launch. **Reversibility:** one-way door in practice.

| # | Cause of failure | Likelihood | Impact | Leading signal / tripwire | Mitigation | Kill criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Free tier cannibalizes paid: customers downgrade rather than convert | H | H | Net new paid MRR growth slows in the first 4 weeks while free sign-ups rise | Gate the top 3 value features behind paid; instrument the free-to-paid funnel before launch | Paid net-new MRR drops below the pre-launch trend for 2 consecutive weeks |
| 2 | Support and infra cost from unqualified free users exceeds plan | H | M | Support tickets per 100 free users above threshold by week 2 | Hard usage caps; self-serve onboarding; a cost-per-free-user budget set before launch | Cost per free user exceeds 1.5x model for 3 weeks with no path to fix |

Note what makes it usable: every row commits to something observable *before* the failure, and to a condition that would make you stop. A list of worries with neither is not a premortem.

<Aside type="tip" title="That artifact is the point">
The deliverable is the register of what could go wrong and what you will do about each, not a vague feeling of caution. Every skill in the library ends in a concrete artifact like this.

If you got an essay instead of a table, see [troubleshooting](/start/troubleshooting/).
</Aside>

Every framework page is layered so you can stop at any depth: a 20-second quick-facts card, the mechanism and procedure, a worked example, then the full evidence dossier with graded sources.
