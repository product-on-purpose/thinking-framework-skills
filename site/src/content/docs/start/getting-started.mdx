This is a library of **thinking frameworks rebuilt as skills** an AI agent can run, and that humans can follow by hand. Each one takes a messy situation and produces a concrete artifact, not a wall of prose: a ranked risk register, a weighted option matrix, an argument map, an honest base-rate estimate.

## Your first framework: a premortem

A [premortem](../../frameworks/think-premortem/) <Badge text="Evidence: S/M" variant="success" /> stress-tests a plan by imagining it has *already failed* and working backward to why, then turning each cause into a tripwire and a pre-decided response.

<Steps>

1. **Pick a real decision** you are about to commit to: a launch, a hire, a migration.

2. **Run the skill** in Claude Code (or follow its numbered steps by hand with a pen):

   ```bash
   /think-premortem "we're about to launch a free tier to drive signups"
   ```

3. **Get a ranked risk register.** For each top risk: a leading signal, a mitigation, an owner, and a kill criterion.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="That artifact is the point">
The deliverable is the register of what could go wrong and what you will do about each, not a vague feeling of caution. Every skill in the library ends in a concrete artifact like this.
</Aside>

## How to read a framework page

Every framework page is layered so you can stop at any depth: a 20-second quick-facts card, the mechanism and procedure, a worked example, then the full evidence dossier with graded sources.
