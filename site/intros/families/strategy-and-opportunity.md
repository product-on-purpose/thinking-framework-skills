label: Strategy & Opportunity
tagline: Test a strategy against the futures you do not control, and locate where the game can actually be changed, instead of optimizing for the single future you happened to assume.
---

These frameworks work on the outside game: not how to frame or decide a bounded problem, but where the opportunity is and whether a direction survives a world that refuses to be predicted. The shared move is to stop treating the future as one forecast to optimize against and start treating it as a space - of forces outside your control, of plausible and divergent worlds - then prefer moves that hold up across that space over bets on one reading of it. Reach here once a problem is framed and options exist, to pressure-test direction before committing to it.
