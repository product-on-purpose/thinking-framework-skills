## Agreement

**Verdict (Build, as a candidate): AGREE.** The engine's mechanism argument holds up against the shipped skills it must clear. `skills/think-decision-option-review/SKILL.md` itself states, in its own "When NOT to Use" list, "To generate options (use an ideation skill); this compares options that already exist" - that is a direct, first-hand textual confirmation that decision-option-review structurally cannot absorb this move without changing its scope, which is the mechanism argument the dossier needed and the prior registry row lacked. The abstraction-laddering comparison is real too: that skill selects one rung on one vertical chain and explicitly disclaims generating solutions ("To generate or choose solutions... use an ideation skill"), while this move enumerates many objectives and generates from each one, never selecting an altitude. A fold into decision-option-review would graft a generation stage onto a skill that disclaims generation by name, and a recipe chain (abstraction-laddering then decision-option-review) genuinely does not reach either the completeness challenge or the per-objective generation step. I would flag the abstraction-laddering overlap ("about a fifth," explicitly "at the edge of the ceiling") as worth a second human look, since the dossier's own words put it at the boundary rather than clearly under it, but the two mechanism differences the dossier names (breadth-first across many objectives vs. depth-first on one chain; never selects an altitude vs. never generates alternatives) are real and substantive, not cosmetic. That is enough for me to agree with Build rather than demand Fold or Recipe.

**Tier (M, narrow): DISAGREE.** The dossier itself names a demotion trigger it cannot discharge: "The grade drops to P if the 2015 [Siebert and Keeney] quality ratings turn out to be participants' self-ratings and no judge-rated quality gain exists elsewhere... The rating method could not be determined from the abstract on 2026-10-20." I spent real effort trying to resolve this first-hand (OpenAlex, Crossref, a WebSearch pass, and an attempted fetch of a ResearchGate PDF that returned 403) and could not resolve it either. The one lead I found - a secondary summary saying "two experimenters analyzed the extent to which an alternative resolved conflicting facets of the problem" in one of the five studies - is not a first-hand reading of the paper and does not clearly describe the *quality* rating the abstract's "number and quality" claim rests on; it is too thin to discharge the trigger. `agents/think-research-framework.md` states a non-negotiable: "When the honest read is split... the tier you emit MUST be the CONSERVATIVE (lower) grade, never the optimistic half: a method whose honest read is 'M/P, transferred' is tier P in the entry, and you state the full split and the transfer in reasoning." The dossier's own table already reads this component as "M, leaning lower" with a named, live, unresolved condition that would flip it to P. Per the library's own rule, that condition should push the governing tier to P *now*, with the split and the open question stated in reasoning exactly as the dossier already does - not held at M "pending" resolution. The `think-consider-the-unknowns` precedent the dossier leans on (M, all-human, single research line, proximal outcome) is a valid floor against going lower to C, but it does not resolve this skill's distinct, named, open self-rating question, because that entry never had an analogous unresolved demotion trigger of its own. My recommendation: ship this candidate at **P, narrow**, with the same split stated in reasoning, until a human reads the Siebert and Keeney (2015) methods section and confirms whether quality was independently judged.

5. **No en/em dashes found.** I scanned both `dossier.md` and `proposed-entry.json` for U+2013/U+2014 and found none; the repo's "no em-dash or en-dash" rule is honored.
6. **Butler, Scherer and Reiter-Palmon (2003) issue number "15(2-3)" is correct, not a typo.** Crossref's `journal-issue` field gives the combined issue "2-3" (OpenAlex's own normalized field shows "2" alone, which would have been a plausible but wrong "simplification" had the dossier used it).
