The catalog grows 56 -> 63 (56 evidence-graded core skills + 7 contested lenses, reported as separate cohorts); the headline eval numbers stay the core-56 numbers.

### Added
- **Contested lenses: the caveat-first contract + seven skills.** Famous-but-weak frameworks can now ship as honest, low-tier, caveat-first skills. A new `caveatFirst` + `posture` + `recommendationPolicy` marker in `frameworks/registry.mjs` (schema-validated) drives a new 9th conformance-gate layer, `scripts/check-contested.mjs` (pure core in `scripts/lib/contested-lib.mjs`, both postures negative-tested in `tests/contested-lib.test.mjs`), which enforces that the deficiency LEADS the SKILL.md and the artifact across `SKILL.md` / `TEMPLATE` / `EXAMPLE` / sample / eval-cases, that a branded lens carries its trademark attribution on every surface, and that the marker agrees across the registry, the SKILL.md frontmatter, and the `skill.meta.yml` sidecar. Seven contested lenses ship. **Run-caveat-first (5):** `think-swot` (X - leads with Hill & Westbrook 1997, then forces the discipline a bare grid lacks plus a TOWS matching step), `think-five-whys` (X - reliable only for simple linear single-cause failures, Card 2017), `think-eisenhower-moscow-pareto` (P - a bundle of three weakly-evidenced presets, leads with the laundering caveat), `think-complexity-domain-sort` (C - the descriptively-named Cynefin sort, trademark-attributed, no controlled validity evidence), `think-reflective-equilibrium` (C - empirically untested as a procedure, runs with an explicit revision ledger). **Warn-and-redirect (2):** `think-analysis-of-competing-hypotheses` (X - leads with the controlled trials and routes to an evidence-based move without reproducing the disconfirmation matrix) and `think-qualitative-comparative-analysis` (P - certifies configurations from noise at session scale, routes to reference-class-forecasting without building the truth table). Catalog 56 -> 63 (56 core + 7 contested lenses), reported as separate cohorts. `cynefin` ships under its descriptive name (`think-complexity-domain-sort`) to keep the trademark out of the invocation; the old `/library/cynefin/` URL redirects.
- **`gen-site.mjs`** renders a "use with caution" admonition + a contested-lens note on a caveat-first framework page; **`gen-catalog.mjs`** carries `caveat_first` / `posture` / `recommendation_policy` into `catalog.json`, `llms-full.txt`, and the evaluated set so an agent reading the catalog sees the posture.
- **Behavioral evals for the 7 contested lenses**, reported as a separate cohort from the core-56: trigger 100% top-1 with **0 false-fires** (no contested lens grabs a generic prompt; every generic strategy/people/prioritization prompt routes to the stronger shipped skill), output 100% (40/40 caveat + artifact checks). Dated scorecards under `docs/internal/eval-results/2026-06-19-contested-{trigger,output}-eval.{md,json}`.

### Changed
- **The advisor is now policy-aware (DS-02).** `gen-recommendable.mjs` carries each skill's `recommendation_policy` + `caveat_first`; `check-registry.mjs`'s shipped == recommendable invariant is policy-aware (a contested lens stays in the corpus but must be flagged `explicit_request_only`); and `think-framework-advisor` will never make a contested lens Step 1 for a generic prompt - it routes a generic "do a SWOT-ish read" to `think-issue-tree` and surfaces the caveat whenever a named lens does appear.

### Added

- **Nine frameworks built from the phase-2 Build survivors** (catalog 47 -> 56 shipped), and a **12th skill-family, Ethics & Values Deliberation** (the first skill-taxonomy family added since Strategy & Opportunity in v0.5.0), anchored by three of them. Each was authored end to end from its vetted research dossier (6-file anatomy, description score 1.0, evidence dossier as the source of truth, honest governing tier carried onto the artifact's pre-printed evidence caveat):
  - `think-veil-of-ignorance-reasoning` (M) - decide a values trade-off as if you had an equal chance of being any affected party, then confront the gap with the positioned decision; the impartiality constraint (identity removal + equiprobability) is empirically distinct from identity-known perspective enumeration. New ethics family.
  - `think-ethical-matrix` (P) - grid affected parties (including voiceless ones) against prima facie principles and read the cell-level trade-off pattern, never a score; the family's anchor artifact-producer. New ethics family.
  - `think-speculative-harms-anti-goals` (A) - assume success at scale, narrate the third-party harm and bad-faith misuse, convert each into a standing anti-goal; the honest A grade (no controlled study) is on its face. New ethics family. The closest-run Build of the batch; a fold into `premortem` is recorded as the defensible alternative.
  - `think-walton-argumentation-schemes` (P) - type a defeasible argument as a stereotyped scheme, then test it with that scheme's keyed critical questions; the sole survivor of the argumentation trio, its scheme-typology engine distinct from shipped `argument-mapping`.
  - `think-dialectical-bootstrapping` (M) - estimate, assume it is wrong and re-estimate from changed assumptions, then average the two numbers; an averaging tail no shipped method owns (Herzog and Hertwig 2009).
  - `think-interval-calibration-check` (P, re-graded from a stale M) - interrogate the width of a stated interval with an equivalent-bet test and hit-rate scoring; distinct from `decision-journal`, which records but never adjusts confidence.
  - `think-consider-the-unknowns` (M) - enumerate the relevant variables you cannot observe and weigh the gap; debias by mapping absent evidence, where every shipped assumption-challenge skill operates on present material (Walters et al. 2017).
  - `think-process-tracing` (P) - adjudicate rival causal explanations of a single case by per-evidence diagnosticity (hoop, smoking-gun, straw-in-the-wind, doubly-decisive); the sole survivor of the causal-inference cluster.
  - `think-interest-based-negotiation` (P) - separate interests from positions, decide against the BATNA inside the mapped ZOPA, invent options for mutual gain; the catalog's first method that models a counterparty.

- The advisor name-safety set is drift-guarded in CI: `scripts/gen-recommendable.mjs --check` runs on PR and push and fails if `recommendable.json` / `recommendable.md` are stale.
- Every CI job now resolves Node from `.nvmrc` (= 24): the `check` (conformance gate) job moved off its hardcoded `node-version: '22'` to `node-version-file: .nvmrc`, matching the site and deploy jobs (family Astro site standard 14.8).
- The site base path is single-sourced in `scripts/site-base.mjs` (family Astro site standard 14.7), consumed by both `site/astro.config.mjs` and the rendered-link guard, so the build and the validator can never disagree on the base.

- Per-file `.md` config sidecars under `site/` (rationale folded into the config files' own comments and a new `site/README.md`).
- The seven remaining repo-level `.md` config/data sidecars (`library.json.md`, `manifest.generated.json.md`, both `plugin.json.md`, both generator `.mjs.md`, and the advisor's `recommendable.json.md`); rationale consolidated into `docs/internal/AUTHORING.md` and the generators' own header comments (family Astro site standard 14.10).

### Fixed
- Docs-site Edit links on hand-authored pages: `editLink.baseUrl` now carries the `/site/` segment (`.../edit/main/site/`) so they resolve to the real repo path instead of 404ing (the Astro project root is `site/`).
- 43 pre-existing browser-broken internal links the new rendered-link guard surfaced on its first run: a bibliography link-depth bug, an over-deep `explore/` index link, and a `by-context` sibling link (`scripts/gen-site.mjs`); two `.mdx` start-page sibling links; and the three base-less 404 hero links (now base-absolute).
- Generated-page "Edit" links no longer 404: `scripts/gen-site.mjs` sets each generated page's `editUrl` to its true source (a per-framework page to its skill's `SKILL.md`) or to `false` for aggregation pages, instead of letting Starlight auto-derive a link to the gitignored build path (family Astro site standard, "Generated-page Edit links").
